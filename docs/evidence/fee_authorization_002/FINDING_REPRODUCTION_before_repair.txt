D5. MUTATION AFTER CONSTRUCTION IS LOAD-BEARING

CONCLUSION: source verification and operator authorization are conflated in `provenance`.
A schedule whose document is verified is automatically `known`, and `known` is what every
downstream gate consults. There is no field that says A PERSON AUTHORIZED THIS ARITHMETIC.
