PREMARKET_CONTEXT_PACKET_V2 — the versioned packet, as actually written by the production CLI

VERSIONING. A V1 packet carries no `schema` field and one `as_of_time` that was doing three jobs. Its
bytes and its original interpretation are preserved; `interpret()` reports its source freshness as
UNRECONSTRUCTIBLE rather than inferring it from the seal clock. V2 separates the jobs and KEEPS
`as_of_time` only with a machine-readable definition attached to every packet.
