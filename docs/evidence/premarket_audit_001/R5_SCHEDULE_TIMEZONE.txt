HOST TIMEZONE EVIDENCE, from the operating system rather than from an assumption:

   date +%Z %z                 -> PDT -0700
   systemsetup -gettimezone    -> requires administrator access; NOT RUN (this audit changes no host settings)

WHAT HAPPENS IF THE HOST MOVES. The generated schedule is bound to America/Los_Angeles in
ops/premarket_repair/timezone_binding.json, which install.sh places at ops/timezone_binding.json. The staged CLI
checks it on every invocation and exits 5 with TIMEZONE_CONFIGURATION_MISMATCH if the live host no longer
matches -- it does not run at a shifted market time.

THE SECOND, INDEPENDENT GUARD. Even with no binding installed at all, a stage decides its disposition from the
MARKET instant, never from the local hour that triggered it. A trigger firing at the wrong local time therefore
lands outside its market window and is refused as TOO_EARLY or MISSED_WINDOW. The binding turns a silent
skip into a named refusal; it is not what prevents the shifted run.

A HOST THAT DOES NOT TRACK THE MARKET ZONE needs more than one local trigger per stage -- a UTC host needs 12:15
in summer and 13:15 in winter for one 08:15 ET target, and StartCalendarInterval cannot express "whichever is
current". The generator emits BOTH; the out-of-season one lands outside the market window and is refused.
