   CONSEQUENCE: EARLY OBSERVATIONS MISREPRESENTED AS FINAL-PREMARKET OBSERVATIONS.

   A CORRECTION TO THE R4 REPORT, which overstated this. The 03:00 ET catalyst
   classifications were NOT intrinsically false: at 03:00 the NVDA, SYND and FUTR filings
   were genuinely not yet knowable, and NO_KNOWN_CATALYST was the correct point-in-time
   answer for that instant. Nothing was fabricated. The defect is that those correct
   EARLY observations were stored under the 09:20-final stage identity and packet context,
   where a reader is entitled to read them as the state of the world at 09:20. The
   misrepresentation is in the LABEL and the CONTEXT, not in the classification.

   WHAT R5 CHANGES ABOUT THIS. Under PREMARKET_CONTEXT_PACKET_V2 the same early packet
   CARRIES ITS OWN CONTRADICTION, because the instants are now separate fields:
      time.packet_data_cutoff        06:00 ET   <- when this packet stopped accepting input
      time.latest_accepted_known_from 06:00 ET  <- the newest thing that actually got in
      time.packet_sealed_at          09:25 ET   <- when it was sealed
