scripts/premarket_run.py lines 143-146, verbatim:
   142 |         target = et().normalize() + pd.Timedelta(hours=h, minutes=m)
   143 |         wait = (target - et()).total_seconds()
   144 |         if wait > 0:
   145 |             time.sleep(min(wait, 3600))

   147 |             last = absorb(label)
