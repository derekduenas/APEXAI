# APEX DISLOCATION TAXONOMY (T1 item 7)

The question behind every class: **when is price behaving unusually
relative to reality?** A dislocation is a MECHANISM claim — someone is
mispositioned, information is repricing, or liquidity has failed — never
a pattern name. Scout may eventually tag these observationally; a class
becomes tradable only through the standard governance path (versioned
playbook, birth, forward evidence). Nothing here is wired into Epoch 1.

Each class states: the observable, the mechanism, and what would make
the tag WRONG.

## 1. PRICE_DISLOCATION (market/sector divergence)
observable: symbol return decouples from market+sector beyond its usual
beta band (canonical RS fields, not a new estimator).
mechanism: idiosyncratic information or forced flow.
wrong when: the "divergence" is a stale/thin print or index rebalance.

## 2. EXTREME_RS
observable: cross-sectional RS percentile pinned at an extreme across
multiple horizons with persistence.
mechanism: sustained one-sided repositioning.
wrong when: driven by one bar; or the whole sector is the mover.

## 3. ABNORMAL_VOLUME
observable: RVOL far above time-of-day baseline WITHOUT proportional
range expansion (participation without progress) or with it (initiative).
mechanism: absorption vs initiative — who is being paid to sit there?
wrong when: known index/ETF flow windows, expiries, rebalances.

## 4. GAP_SHOCK
observable: open far outside prior range; behavior vs gap edge decides
(hold = acceptance, fill = rejection).
mechanism: overnight information repricing; trapped overnight positioning.
wrong when: the gap is a corporate action artifact (split/dividend).

## 5. LIQUIDITY_VACUUM
observable: spread blowout + depth withdrawal (microscope L2) while
price travels far on little volume.
mechanism: makers pulled; price discovering, not trading.
wrong when: the book snapshot is stale (book health must be OK).

## 6. FAILED_AUCTION
observable: push beyond a reference level that cannot hold acceptance;
immediate re-entry into prior range.
mechanism: the breakout cohort is trapped; their exits are fuel.
wrong when: the "level" was not structurally meaningful (mid-range noise).

## 7. EVENT_REPRICING
observable: KNOWN_CATALYST (Event Eyes) + abnormal volume + range.
mechanism: the market digesting a filing/announcement.
wrong when: catalyst attribution is EVENT_UNCERTAIN — then this tag is
FORBIDDEN, not assumed.

## 8. VOLATILITY_DISLOCATION
observable: realized range vs ATR context at an extreme (compression at
lows = energy stored; serial expansion = energy spent).
mechanism: regime transition in progress.
wrong when: a single monster bar dominates the estimate.

## 9. OPTIONS_UNDERLYING_DISAGREEMENT (future: needs options surface)
observable: option-implied move materially disagrees with realized
behavior on the candidate set.
mechanism: one market knows something the other has not priced.
wrong when: quotes are stale/wide (option quote quality gates first).

## 10. TRAPPED_PARTICIPANT_UNWIND
observable: failed break + reclaim + acceleration through the trap zone
(ParticipantTrapState from the Visual/numeric eyes).
mechanism: forced exits become the other side's entry.
wrong when: "trap" inferred from one wick rather than acceptance shape.

## 11. CROSS_ASSET_CONTRADICTION (future: needs cross-asset fabric)
observable: symbol ignores a large correlated-asset move it historically
tracks.
mechanism: pending idiosyncratic information or a stale leg.
wrong when: the correlation itself has regime-shifted (Twin transition).

## Standing law

A dislocation tag is an INVITATION for the Hunter to ask "what is the
mechanism?", never a signal. Tags carry decision_power
NONE_OBSERVATIONAL_EPOCH1 until a versioned playbook earns otherwise
with forward evidence.
