# THE APEX CAPTAIN — CIO / META-BRAIN (doctrine + constitution, 2026-08-16)

The Captain is the missing ORGANIZATIONAL layer, not another prediction
engine. It sits OVER the process, not inline as another filter.

    Scout:    what is unusual?
    Hunter:   is there a mechanism?
    Oracle:   what is likely to happen?
    Assassin: why are we wrong?
    Capital:  does it deserve money?
    CAPTAIN:  given everything APEX knows, what should the machine do next?

## THE CONSTITUTION (executable law — tests/test_captain.py)

    Captain commands the desk. Capital commands the money.

- The Captain may never authorize, size, price, or move a stop. Its
  kernel contains no path to any of them (asserted by source scan).
- Risk can veto the Captain. The Assassin can wound its thesis. Capital
  can refuse its best-ranked opportunity. The trade manager owns the
  position. Execution can refuse an order intent.
- The Claude CIO advises; it can never rule. `apply_to_kernel()` returns
  the kernel directive UNMODIFIED with advice attached as commentary,
  and a CIO record that claims authority raises at construction.
- **Math has authority. Claude has judgment.**

## DOCTRINE (the personality — a doctrine, not a persona)

PATIENT — does not need action. PREDATORY — when genuine asymmetry
appears, attends immediately. SKEPTICAL — assumes attractive trades hide
failure modes. PROBABILITY-MINDED — never confuses a compelling story
with a high probability. CONTEXTUAL — no instrument exists outside its
world. PRICE-SENSITIVE — a great company is a terrible trade at the
wrong entry. ADAPTIVE — changes beliefs when the world changes.
CAPITAL-PRESERVING — survival outranks opportunity. DECISIVE — when
evidence, execution, and risk genuinely align, it says PROCEED; an
anti-paralysis duty implemented as dead code is a bug, which is why the
PROCEED tier is reachable in the CURRENT epoch, not only in a
hypothetical future one.

## CONVICTION STATE (structured, never a magic score)

Twelve typed dimensions (mechanism, market fit, analog support, ML
evidence, calibration, scenario shape, assassin, entry quality,
execution, tail risk, portfolio fit, disagreement), each STRONG /
MODERATE / WEAK / UNKNOWN — and UNKNOWN is a real answer that never
silently becomes MODERATE. A single 0-100 score would launder twelve
kinds of ignorance into one confident digit; the Captain's most
valuable sentence — *"direction good, entry bad, wait"* — is impossible
to say with a scalar.

ORDINARY_OPPORTUNITY vs EXCEPTIONAL_ASYMMETRY: the rare-dislocation
class requires strong mechanism AND asymmetric scenario shape AND clean
Assassin AND strong entry AND acceptable tails AND low disagreement AND
non-UNKNOWN calibration. In Epoch 1 it is structurally unreachable —
correct, because APEX cannot yet KNOW it is looking at a rare
dislocation. (The operating lesson worth borrowing from famous
dislocation traders is recognizing when price and reality disconnect;
their exact methods are not publicly reproducible and APEX does not
pretend to clone them.)

## THE OPPORTUNITY BOARD

Candidates do not act independently; the board answers the professional
question — *if one unit of risk is available, which opportunity deserves
it?* Ranking is lexicographic on structured evidence (compromised last,
then quality tier, entry quality, fewer unknowns, deterministic
tiebreak), never a blended score, so an ordering is always explicable.
Rejected candidates stay ON the board with reasons: a board of survivors
only is a survivor gallery. **Ranking is process; allocation is money.**

## INTELLIGENCE SPEND

The Captain buys thinking in proportion to how long the edge plausibly
survives (edge-persistence routing) and never buys deep research for a
compromised or unassessable thesis. Routing can only REDUCE spend.

## LIFECYCLE

Before entry: what should we do next? During: does the world that
justified this position still exist (comparison only — the deterministic
manager acts)? After: forensic review behind a context wall (what did we
believe, what happened, which component was wrong, did the Assassin
warn, did Capital underprice risk, did execution consume the edge) →
Research Memory. Over hundreds of observations this becomes APEX's
institutional memory of itself.

## EPOCH 1 STATUS: OBSERVATIONAL

`captain_state` records are written beside decisions and change no Epoch
1 outcome (`decision_power: NONE_OBSERVATIONAL_EPOCH1`). Wiring the
Captain's directives into live routing is a versioned ruling for a later
epoch. The freeze holds; the layer accumulates evidence about its own
judgment in the meantime — and will be ablated like every other seat.
