The operator's ruling, recorded verbatim in intent: APEX does not become a
top-1% trader because it has AI. It earns the label only by demonstrating,
on prospective forward evidence, all of the following. This definition is

## The ten dimensions (all required; none tradeable against another)

1. **Selection** — chosen opportunities materially outperform rejected
   opportunities.
2. **Prediction** — forecasts show genuine out-of-sample discrimination.
3. **Calibration** — when APEX says ~70%, reality behaves like ~70%.
4. **Asymmetry** — winners meaningfully outweigh losers after costs.
5. **Risk** — no catastrophic drawdowns; survival binds before return.
6. **Selectivity** — it does not need to trade constantly; NO-TRADE is a
   first-class output.
7. **Adaptation** — viable across market conditions without hindsight
   retuning.
8. **Execution** — expected edge survives spread/slippage/fills.
9. **Capital allocation** — more capital flows to legitimately stronger
   opportunities without recklessly increasing probability of ruin.
10. **Durability** — not AMD/NVDA + one month + one regime carrying
    everything (concentration diagnostics must clear).

## The three relationships the forward ledger must answer first

R1. **Hunter vs scanner**: do playbook-selected names outperform merely
    abnormal names? If not, the playbooks add no intelligence.
R2. **Hunter vs baselines**: does APEX beat raw momentum, raw relative
    strength, market direction, always-take-candidate? If not, the
    sophistication is not earning its keep.
R3. **Selected vs rejected**: is there economic separation between what
    APEX takes and what it declines? An elite selector shows separation;
    measurement isolates selection by scoring BOTH cohorts under the SAME
    naive instrument (BASELINE-MOMENTUM), so playbook direction skill and
    subject-selection skill are not conflated.

All three are computed mechanically by the scoreboard from forward-
eligible records only. They are DESCRIPTIVE until the protocol §10
checkpoint (≥40 sessions AND ≥100 effective observations across ≥2 regime
states); before that every number carries PRELIMINARY.
