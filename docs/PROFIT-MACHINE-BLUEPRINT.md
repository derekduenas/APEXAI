## The Assassin doctrine (formalized)

The Assassin is a LOGICAL STAGE, not one agent: Claude attacks
qualitatively (Adversarial Trader), quant attacks statistically
(disagreement, analogue support), data attacks structurally (quality),
capital attacks economically, risk attacks existentially. **Anything can
attack the trade; nothing except the complete pipeline can authorize
it.** Every attempt — landed or not — is a ledger record, because
rejection economics is unmeasurable otherwise. Epoch 1 semantics: the
Assassin wounds (monotone caution; WATCH cap), capital executes kills;
lethal qualitative force is a deferred versioned ruling awaiting forward
evidence that Assassin objections identify genuinely worse outcomes.

## Rejection economics (the Profit Machine thesis)

Every filter must make the remaining population harder and better. The
scoreboard's funnel_economics section reports, per stage cohort under
the SAME naive instrument: scanner watchlist → playbook matched →
assassin survived-clean → capital OBSERVE, each with N and 60m EV.
Doctrine: a stage that adds no EV is deleted prestige; a stage that
subtracts EV is actively hurting. We delete prestige. We keep edge.
